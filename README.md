## Executive Overview
DraftLineup.com provides an enterprise-grade hockey tactical, developmental, and talent evaluation operating system:

- **DraftLineup Command Hub**: Universal dashboard with live player telemetry, draft board tracking, and scout dispatches.
- **Lifelong Athlete Continuum**: Spanning grassroots youth development (USA Hockey ADM U8-U14), elite high school and prep academies, junior hockey (USHL, NAHL, BCHL, CHL), collegiate powerhouses (NCAA D1 & D3), and minor pro leagues (AHL, ECHL).
- **Master Scouting Directory**: 3,770+ verified athletes, head coaches, general managers, scouts, and 61 overseas professional clubs with instant filtering and CSV/JSON export.
- **NIL Valuation & Transfer Portal War Room**: Live collegiate transfer portal intelligence with DraftLineup NIL Index™ market valuation ($5k to $250k+/yr) and 1-click recruiting DMs.
- **AI Scouting Assistant Co-Pilot (`Ctrl+J`)**: Instant natural language scouting answers across athlete dossiers, club registries, and trade machine pick valuations.
- **3D Virtual Rink & Practice Simulator**: WebGL spatial simulation featuring multi-camera switcher, goalie sightline cones, and tactical playbook execution.
- **Enterprise Video Telestration Studio**: 60 FPS simulated broadcast analyzer with optical flow velocity telemetry and 1-click recruiting highlight reel creation.
- **Draft Board & Trade Simulator**: 32-franchise Entry Draft war room featuring an empirical pick value trade machine (Rich Hill / Jimmy Johnson hockey curve).
- **Executive Scouting Dossier Generator**: One-click high-resolution PDF/Print export for verified player passports and scout evaluations.
- **Global Command Palette (`Ctrl + K`)**: Instant keyboard navigation, search across 3,770+ athletes, 61 overseas clubs, and module shortcuts on every page.
