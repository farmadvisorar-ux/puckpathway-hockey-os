## Executive Overview
BlueLine DataWorks provides an enterprise-grade hockey tactical, developmental, and talent evaluation operating system:
- **Lifelong Athlete Continuum**: Spanning grassroots youth development (USA Hockey ADM U8-U14), elite high school and prep academies, junior hockey (USHL, NAHL, BCHL, CHL), collegiate powerhouses (NCAA D1 Men & Women, NCAA D3, ACHA), and minor pro leagues (AHL, ECHL) with strictly non-NHL player registries.
- **Master Scouting Directory**: 2,974 verified athletes, head coaches, general managers, and scouts with instant filtering, search, and CSV/JSON data export.
- **Head of Scouting Operations**: Configured with **Director of Scouting** as the lead recruiting executive with signed audit ledgers and evaluation stamps.
- **Real-Time Broadcast Voice AI**: Speech-to-stats commentary engine, NLP entity extraction, real-time momentum graphing, and voice debriefs.
- **The BlueLine Wire ("X for Hockey")**: Dedicated public social platform for hockey athletes, scouts, coaches, and organizations with rich posts, commitment announcements, trending hashtags, and live player telemetry.
- **Universal Peer-to-Peer Messaging**: Real-time direct communication between players, coaches, teams, and recruiters with verified badges and icebreaker prompts.
- **Gamified "PuckXP" & Level-Up Tracker**: Athletes progress from Grassroots Rookie (Lv. 1) to NCAA D1 / Draft Eligible (Lv. 99) through training quests and verified milestones.
- **24-Agent Autonomous Data Harvesting Swarm**: 24 synchronized AI agents (4 specialized clones across each of the 6 intelligence archetypes) operating on a 1-hour continuous staggered relay. Each hour, a new agent goes online as Lead Pilot while the agent that started last hour immediately steps into the Co-Pilot seat, sharing an immutable collective memory to trial-probe 40+ open source feeds, rank signal quality (0-100 SQM), and auto-compile master lifelong player trajectories every 12 hours.
- **Tactical Telestration & 2D Whiteboard**: Full-ice and half-ice interactive drill designer with customizable play sequences, passing routes, and cone layouts.
- **NCAA Transfer Portal & NIL Valuation War Room**: Live collegiate and junior transfer portal intelligence with NCAA recruiting calendar status (Contact, Quiet, Dead periods), BlueLine NIL Index™ algorithmic market valuation ($5k to $250k+/yr), interactive NIL simulator, and 1-click encrypted recruiting DMs connecting college coaches directly to student-athletes.
- **Enterprise AI Video Telestration & Tactical Film Studio**: 60 FPS simulated broadcast and custom video analyzer with computer vision skater detection bounding boxes, optical flow speed telemetry (MPH), puck velocity trail tracking, defensive gap rulers, pro telestration suite (Spotlights, Vectors, Passing Seams, Rulers), and 1-click 60-second recruiting highlight reel generation with auto-broadcasting to The BlueLine Wire.
- **3D Interactive Virtual Rink & Practice Simulator**: High-performance Three.js WebGL spatial simulation featuring multi-camera switcher (Broadcast 45°, Overhead 90°, Skater First-Person POV, and Goalie Crease), dynamic Goalie Sightline & Coverage Cone (visualizing square angle vs high-danger exposure), full regulation 3D boards/ice/nets, tactical playbook systems, and 1-click export to The BlueLine Wire (#Tactical3D).
- **Live Multi-Game Scouting War Room & Game Center**: Real-time quad-game scorebug switcher, period-by-period shot telemetry breakdowns, live play-by-play shift stream with real-time on-ice athlete tracking, and 1-click scouting report dispatch directly to The BlueLine Wire (`#GameNight`).
- **Enterprise Draft Board & Mock Draft Simulator (`draft.html`)**: 32-franchise NHL and USHL Entry Draft war room featuring an algorithmic Top Prospect Big Board (derived from lifelong development & feeder pedigree), an empirical pick value trade machine (Rich Hill / Jimmy Johnson hockey curve), customizable simulation clocks, franchise positional needs matrix, and 1-click dispatch to The BlueLine Wire (`#DraftRoom`).
- **Rich Social Preview Engine**: Open Graph and Twitter Card metadata configured with high-definition branding and the official BlueLine DataWorks emblem for SMS, iMessage, WhatsApp, email, Twitter/X, Discord, and Slack link sharing.
