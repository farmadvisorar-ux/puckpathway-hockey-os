Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding BlueLine DataWorks

What makes BlueLine DataWorks different from existing scouting databases? ▾

Existing tools only show snapshots at a single point in time (e.g. just draft year or just college stats). BlueLine DataWorks is the only platform that tracks athletes from their earliest competitive stages through professional careers, giving a continuous 360° view of evolution, biometric test percentiles, and academic eligibility.

How does the cryptographic genesis ledger protect athlete data? ▾

Every biometric test, combine record, and profile claim generates an HMAC-SHA256 hash block. This ensures that verified combine numbers (such as 1080 Sprint speeds or vertical jump telemetry) cannot be retroactively altered, fabricated, or forged.

Can amateur athletes use BlueLine DataWorks without violating NCAA amateurism? ▾

Yes, 100%. BlueLine DataWorks is an analytics, combine tracking, and recruiting intelligence operating system. It complies strictly with NCAA bylaws regarding amateurism and recruitment tracking.

How does the 24-agent crawler swarm update data? ▾

Our mesh swarm operates 24 autonomous ingestion agents monitoring 52 live channels across NCAA, USHL, CHL, InStat, and Catapult telemetry. Data updates automatically with sub-second latency.
