01. Commitment to Privacy & Platform Governance

DraftLineup.com ("DraftLineup", "we", "us", or "our") operates the premier sports analytics, hockey scouting intelligence, and athletic trajectory modeling ecosystem. We respect the personal privacy of all individuals who interact with our services, including athletes, scouts, coaches, organizational front offices, and parents.

This Privacy Policy explains the nature, scope, purpose, and lawful bases under which DraftLineup collects, processes, stores, and protects personal data and athletic telemetry, as well as the rights available to athletes and parents under federal and international data protection laws.

02. Children's & Minor Athlete Data Protection Charter (Under 18)

We adhere to strict standards governing the privacy and protection of young athletes under the age of 18 pursuant to:

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA, 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506): DraftLineup does not knowingly collect personal contact information (such as personal phone numbers, physical home addresses, or private messaging accounts) directly from children under 13 without verified parental consent.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act & California Age-Appropriate Design Code Act (AB 2273): Minor athletes under 18 possess heightened privacy safeguards. We do not sell or share the personal information of minors under 16 without affirmative parental authorization.
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR Article 8 & UK GDPR): For youth athletes residing in the European Union or United Kingdom, processing of youth information requires lawful parental consent where required by national law.
  • Dedicated Parental Opt-Out Portal: Parents or legal guardians have the unilateral authority to request the immediate suppression, anonymization, or complete deletion of their child's athletic profile, box score histories, and combine telemetry via our verified Under-18 Minor Athlete Opt-Out Form.

03. Categories of Information We Process

We process information falling into three distinct operational categories:

A. Public Athletic Records

Box scores, goals, assists, penalty minutes, plus/minus ratings, team rosters, draft positions, tournament results, and games played published by official sanctioned leagues, amateur federations, and governing bodies (e.g., USA Hockey, Hockey Canada, CHL, USHL, NCAA, IIHF).

B. Telemetry & Performance Metrics

On-ice velocity, shot release speed, shift length distribution, combine metrics (vertical jump, pro agility shuttle, bench press reps), and AI-generated trajectory ratings calculated from algorithmic models or verified combine submissions.

C. Account & Subscriber Data

Names, professional email addresses, organizational affiliation (e.g., NHL team, junior club, university scouting staff), authentication tokens, and billing transaction identifiers for institutional and parent subscribers.

04. Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA) Compliance

In accordance with the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (740 ILCS 14/) and analogous state biometric privacy statutes:

  • No Facial Recognition or Retinal Scans: DraftLineup does NOT scan, capture, generate, or store biometric identifiers including facial geometric maps, retina or iris scans, fingerprints, or voiceprints.
  • Kinematic Telemetry Distinction: Physical performance metrics derived from standard video analysis or optical tracking (such as stride frequency, puck exit velocity, or acceleration rates) represent kinematic event telemetry and are not used to physically authenticate individuals.
  • Biometric Consent: In the event that advanced physiological telemetry (e.g., heart rate variability monitors or wearable sensor telemetry) is ingested, such data is processed solely under express opt-in consent from the athlete or their legal guardian, and will be permanently purged upon written request.

05. Purposes and Lawful Bases of Processing

DraftLineup processes information exclusively for legitimate sports analytics and scouting purposes:

  • To maintain comprehensive, historical athletic progression databases across amateur and professional hockey.
  • To generate algorithmic developmental projections, draft probability tiers, and peer cohort comparisons.
  • To facilitate scouting discovery and talent evaluation by verified university programs, junior franchises, and professional scouting staffs.
  • To enforce site security, prevent unauthorized automated scraping, and maintain data integrity.
  • To process customer subscriptions, account verifications, and compliance takedown requests.

06. Absolute Non-Sale of Personal Data

DraftLineup does not sell, rent, lease, or monetize athlete personal information or minor data to third-party advertising brokers, commercial marketing networks, or consumer profiling agencies.

Data is shared strictly within authorized operational parameters:

  • Verified Evaluators: Vetted scouts, coaches, and recruiters accessing platform analytics under enterprise agreements.
  • Technical Service Providers: Trusted hosting providers, database infrastructures, and transactional email gateways (e.g., Zoho Mail, Google Cloud) operating under confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal & Regulatory Mandates: Where required by valid subpoena, court order, or applicable law enforcement process.

07. Your Legal Rights: Access, Correction, and Erasure

Depending on your jurisdiction, you or your parent/legal guardian possess fundamental statutory privacy rights:

  • Right to Know & Access: You may request a copy of all personal records and telemetry associated with your name or profile.
  • Right to Rectification: You may submit verified box score corrections or roster updates to ensure accuracy.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): Athletes (or parents of athletes under 18) may demand the complete removal of their personal profile, biometric data, and media clips from DraftLineup.com.
  • Processing Timelines: Verified parental takedown requests submitted through our Minor Opt-Out Portal or sent to hello@draftkings.com are acknowledged immediately and permanently executed within forty-eight (48) hours.

08. Cookies, Tracking & Security Shield Architecture

DraftLineup utilizes essential session cookies and local storage tokens to preserve user login sessions, verify role permissions, and maintain platform preferences. We deploy the security_shield.js active runtime guard to detect malicious bots, enforce cross-site request forgery (CSRF) defenses, and prevent illicit automated harvesting of youth athlete directories.

09. Data Retention & Security Standards

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest) to safeguard stored records. When a profile takedown request is approved, the athlete's record is immediately removed from public querying indexes, internal caches are flushed, and suppression headers are dispatched to prevent search engine re-indexing.
