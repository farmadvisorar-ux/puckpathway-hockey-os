Scouting, Recruiting & NIL Legal Disclaimers

Essential regulatory notices regarding NCAA recruiting compliance (Bylaw Article 13), amateurism preservation, statistical projection models, and independent journalistic fair use.

01. NCAA Recruiting Service Compliance (Bylaw Article 13 & 13.14.3)

DraftLineup.com operates strictly in compliance with NCAA regulations governing recruiting and scouting services:

  • Universal Commercial Availability: In accordance with NCAA Bylaw 13.14.3, our scouting reports, database directories, and analytical tools are publicly and commercially available to all NCAA institutions, member colleges, junior programs, and the public on identical subscription terms.
  • Geographic and Open Scope: The platform does not provide customized, proprietary recruiting endorsements, steering services, or exclusive access intended to advantage a specific institution over another.
  • Fee Structure Standard: Subscription pricing is standardized and published publicly; no fee is charged to student-athletes or their families as a condition of being evaluated, indexed, or ranked.

02. Student-Athlete Amateurism & Non-Agency Disclosure

DraftLineup.com is an independent analytics platform and scouting directory. We do NOT act as an athlete agent, broker, sports marketer, NIL collective, or player representative under NCAA Bylaw 12 or relevant state uniform athlete agent statutes (UAAA).

Neither inclusion in our public player database nor subscription to our parent/scout services creates an agency relationship. We do not negotiate professional contracts, handle NIL commercial agreements, or market an athlete's playing ability to professional franchises in violation of amateurism eligibility rules.

03. Name, Image, and Likeness (NIL) Policy & State Regulations

For student-athletes participating in collegiate, junior, or high school competition:

  • High School Athletes (NFHS & State Associations): Many state high school athletic associations strictly prohibit interscholastic athletes from monetizing their NIL while enrolled. DraftLineup does NOT offer commercial NIL compensation, endorsements, or financial sponsorships to amateur student-athletes.
  • Collegiate Athletes: Any athlete managing their own verified profile must comply with their institution’s compliance office guidelines, disclosure requirements, and state NIL legislation.
  • Parental Safeguards: Parents of minors under 18 who wish to prevent public display of their child’s athletic metrics may submit an expedited takedown via our Minor Athlete Opt-Out Form.

04. Algorithmic Projections & Trajectory Modeling Disclaimer

All predictive metrics featured on DraftLineup.com — including Projected Draft Round, NHL Similarity Cohorts, Star Potential Ratings, Win Above Replacement (WAR) projections, and Speed Ratings — are derived from proprietary statistical modeling and machine learning algorithms analyzing historical comparative data.

NO WARRANTY OR GUARANTEE: Trajectory projections are purely informative statistical simulations. DraftLineup makes no representations, warranties, or guarantees that any athlete will achieve a projected draft position, receive an athletic scholarship, secure a professional contract, or reach any specified performance milestone.

05. Athletic Telemetry & Non-Medical Assessment Notice

Combine scores, sprint timings, vertical leap measurements, and physiological telemetry displayed on DraftLineup are intended solely for athletic conditioning, training assessment, and comparative sports performance analysis.

These metrics do not constitute medical, orthopedic, or physiological health advice. Athletes and parents must consult licensed medical professionals, certified athletic trainers, and healthcare providers before undertaking any rigorous athletic training or conditioning regime.
