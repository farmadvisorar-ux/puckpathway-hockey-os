BlueLine DataWorks Terms of Service

Enterprise End-User License Agreement & Roster Eligibility Protocol

1. Non-NHL Amateur Athlete Mandate

BlueLine DataWorks is purpose-built exclusively to track amateur, prep, high school, junior (USHL, NAHL, BCHL, CHL), and collegiate (NCAA Division I/III, ACHA) student-athletes. By utilizing this service, organizations agree to preserve amateur eligibility guardrails in compliance with collegiate athletic associations.

2. Role-Based Subscriptions & Pricing

Subscribers to the Scout & Coach Tier ($22.99/month or $220/annual) receive full combined access to all 22+ analytical modules, including the Lead Scout Desk, advanced micro-telemetry, recruiting boards, and The Wire direct messaging. Subscribers to the Player & Family Tier ($4.99/month) receive athlete passport verification, development progress tracking, and full interactive access to The Wire community with crest stamping.

3. Immutable Ledger & Biometric Verification

All biometric test scores recorded in the Combine Lab and player dossiers are stamped into an immutable audit ledger with cryptographic block hashing. False attestation of combine numbers or identity impersonation will result in immediate credential revocation.

4. Autonomous Swarm Web Ingestion

BlueLine DataWorks operates an autonomous 24-agent crawler swarm that queries open-source hockey registries and tournament schedules. Unauthorized extraction, resale, or reverse-engineering of BlueLine’s proprietary Signal Quality Metrics (SQM) is strictly prohibited.

Student-Athlete & Family Privacy Policy

FERPA & COPPA Protection for Minor and Collegiate Athletes

1. Protection of Minor Athlete Records (COPPA)

For student-athletes under 18 years of age, developmental metrics, academic eligibility markers, and personal communications are protected with parental/guardian oversight keys. Contact information is shielded from public unauthenticated scraping.

2. No Third-Party Data Broker Resale

BlueLine DataWorks does not sell, rent, or trade student-athlete dossiers, combine bio-metrics, or scouting notes to third-party ad networks or commercial brokers. Data remains strictly within authorized scout, coach, and family circles.

3. Network Telemetry & Shield Defenses

Inbound connections are monitored via our Cyber Shield RASP (Runtime Application Self-Protection) module to detect malicious scraping bots, credential stuffing, and IP anomalies. All telemetry logs are encrypted at rest with AES-256.

4. Right to Dossier Correction & Claiming

Athletes and families retain the right to verify, claim, and request corrections to their historical statistics, tournament rosters, or bio-mechanical benchmarks via the Player Passport portal.
